# IELTS Weekly Study Planner

Use this plan for four weeks. Adjust the daily time to your schedule and record your scores after every timed activity.

## Monday — Listening

- Complete one Listening section under timed conditions.
- Review every incorrect answer and note whether the cause was spelling, distraction, vocabulary, or prediction.
- Replay difficult sections and shadow the speaker for 10 minutes.

## Tuesday — Reading

- Complete one passage in 20 minutes.
- Mark the keywords in each question and find their paraphrases in the passage.
- Add five useful words or phrases to your vocabulary notebook.

## Wednesday — Writing

- Plan one Task 2 response in 10 minutes.
- Write the full response in 40 minutes.
- Check paragraph purpose, linking, examples, grammar, and word count.

## Thursday — Speaking

- Record answers to five Part 1 questions.
- Prepare for one minute and record one Part 2 answer.
- Listen again and identify repetition, hesitation, grammar errors, and unclear pronunciation.

## Friday — Mixed review

- Redo questions you previously answered incorrectly.
- Review this week's vocabulary.
- Rewrite one weak paragraph or rerecord one weak speaking answer.

## Saturday — Mock test

- Complete one timed module in exam conditions.
- Record your raw score and estimated band.
- Choose one specific improvement target for next week.
